01 / Overview

Planned ahead.
Streamed in real time.

Plan2Stream is a two-stage system for real-time, long video and audio–video streaming. A planner continuously plans ahead of synthesis, producing compact visual or joint audio–visual plans at half the output resolution and frame rate. Synthesis begins as soon as the first plan chunk is ready, and each short synthesis chunk receives guidance from planned content beyond its own span throughout denoising.

Real-time

58FPS

End-to-end streaming.

58 FPS at 640×352 and 24 FPS at 960×544 on four H100 GPUs. Both stages are jointly distilled for four-step generation, and a distributed runtime overlaps planning, synthesis and decoding.

04 / Method

Planning and synthesis,
running in parallel.

Interactive 3D schematic · drag to rotate · pinch or Ctrl+scroll to zoom · open full screen ↗

01

Continuous planning

The planner generates compact visual or joint audio–visual plans at half the output resolution and frame rate, jointly modeling longer intervals with fewer tokens.

02

Training-Aligned Rolling Guidance

Synthesis is guided by planned content beyond each chunk through bounded plan windows, keeping the local conditioning layout consistent between training and inference.

03

Plan-Aligned RoPE

Maintains temporal alignment between planning and synthesis within bounded positional coordinates as the plan windows advance.

04

Hybrid on-policy distillation

Both stages are jointly distilled for four-step generation with an on-policy hybrid objective, unified through their conditional moments.

05

Long rollout training

Training extends to long self-generated rollouts (32 s) that use the same plan updates and history caching as streaming inference.

06

Overlapped runtime

Planning keeps its own GPU while synthesis is parallelized across the rest; planning, synthesis and decoding overlap for continuous output.
